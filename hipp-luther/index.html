Read and HIPP the source

The Ninety-five Theses Martin Luther · Wittenberg, 1517 · adapted excerpts

A note before you read: Luther wrote this in Latin about 500 years ago, and even the English translations are hard to read today. These excerpts have been rewritten in everyday English so you can focus on HIPP. The ideas are Luther's; only the wording has been made easier. Historians call this an adapted source.

Martin Luther, 1517. Originally written in Latin. Adapted from the English translation in Works of Martin Luther, vol. 1 (Philadelphia: A. J. Holman, 1915). 8 of the original 95 theses shown. Tap a word with a dotted underline to see what it means.
