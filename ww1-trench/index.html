Inside a WWI Trench

A British front line on the Western Front, about 1916

For most of the First World War, two lines of trenches faced each other across Belgium and France. You are looking from behind the British lines toward the German trenches on the far side of no man’s land.

The ground beside one fire bay has been cut away so you can see the trench from the side, and the dugout hidden beneath it. The model shows no people. Millions of men lived, worked, and died in trenches like these.
