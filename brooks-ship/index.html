Inside a Slave Ship

The Brooks of Liverpool, as measured for Parliament in 1788

This is a cutaway model of a real ship that carried enslaved Africans across the Atlantic. The near side has been removed so you can see the decks, rooms, and hold inside.

The model does not show people. Each gold outline below deck marks the space the law allowed one person. Every outline stood for someone with a name, a family, and a home.
