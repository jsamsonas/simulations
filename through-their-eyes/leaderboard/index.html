Each student’s first game counts. These are the students’ own wars, as privates in the regiment of the soldier they followed. Rhodes, Gooding, Edmonds and Ransom put students in the Union army; Watkins and Stanley put them in the Confederate army. A soldier can appear in more than one column: someone wounded at Antietam who later died of disease counts as both wounded and died of disease. “Wounded” and “Captured” include everyone who was ever wounded or captured.
