3 · Same four questions, any source

Remember the conquistador's letter from the HIPP video? HIPP works the same way on a letter as on a stone. Only the clues change.

The Sun Stone (an object)The conquistador's letter, 1519 (a document)
HCarved in Tenochtitlan in the early 1500s, when the Mexica empire was at its height.Written in 1519 during a bloody conquest, not the peaceful surrender it claims.
IMexica nobles, priests, crowds at ceremonies, visitors from conquered peoples, and the gods.The King of Spain, who paid for the expedition and expected a return.
PTo honor the sun and show that the empire and its ruler kept the world running.To convince the king the expedition was worth it and would bring future wealth.
PThe view of the Mexica ruler and priests. Conquered peoples and captives aren't heard.The view of a conquistador seeking personal glory. The people he conquered aren't heard.
Clues come from: images, size, material, where it stoodClues come from: words, who signed it, who it's addressed to, word choice
