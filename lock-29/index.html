Lock 29 at Peninsula

The Ohio & Erie Canal in the Cuyahoga Valley, mid-1800s

For about 80 years, canal boats climbed and dropped through this stone lock in Peninsula, Ohio, then crossed the Cuyahoga River on a wooden aqueduct. The lock is still there, along the Towpath Trail in Cuyahoga Valley National Park.

North is toward Cleveland and Lake Erie, downhill. South is toward Akron, uphill. A lock is how a boat climbed that hill.
