The Transportation Revolution

How Americans got moving, 1807 to the 1850s

In 1800, hauling goods across the Appalachian Mountains meant weeks of slow, muddy travel by wagon. Your notes call what came next the Transportation Revolution: a period of fast growth in how people and goods moved across the United States. Thanks to new inventions, travel became fast, cheap, and reliable for the first time.

This tour visits the four big developments from your notes. Start with stop 1 and work your way through, or pick any stop on the map.
